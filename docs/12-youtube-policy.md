# 12. YouTube policy we design around

Researched Nov 2026.

- **AI use is not banned or demonetised by itself.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (renamed July 2025, clarified July 2026) demonetises **templated, interchangeable, mass-produced** content, shock bait, and AI personas of real people on sensitive topics. It is judged **channel-wide**.
- Explicitly allowed: "using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented".
- In January 2026 YouTube terminated 16 AI channels (~35M subscribers), including AI-animated story channels that used other people's IP. Kids' AI content gets the heaviest scrutiny.
- Clearly animated fiction **does not need** the synthetic-content label. The label doesn't affect pay.
- RPM depends on **viewer** geography, not the creator's (the owner is in Bulgaria). Long-form entertainment ~$2-7 RPM; Shorts ~$0.05-0.50.

## What this means for every episode

- Each story is original, with a real arc, real research and a recognisable house style. Don't produce near-identical videos from one template.
- Moderate volume (about 2 long videos a week), with a human review step.
- Own or properly license every audio and visual input. No game footage, logos, characters or music from others (e.g. never Rockstar/GTA assets even for trend topics).
- Never "made for kids". Keep the tone teen/adult.
- Upload through the official YouTube API only.
